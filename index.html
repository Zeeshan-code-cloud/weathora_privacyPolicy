Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how Weathora handles information when you check the weather and explore cities.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Weathora mobile application. Please review it before using the app. Update this policy whenever the app's data practices change.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, Weathora may process the following information:

  • City searches: City names you enter to find local weather forecasts.
  • Location coordinates: Latitude and longitude used to retrieve weather for a selected city or, if implemented and permitted, your device location.
  • App preferences: Settings such as temperature units, theme, language, or saved cities, if those features are enabled.
  • Technical information: Limited diagnostic or usage information may be processed by the app platform or third-party services, if integrated.

Weathora does not require you to create an account to use basic weather features, unless a future version introduces an account-based feature.

2. Location Permission

You can search for a city manually. If Weathora offers current-location weather, it may request permission to access your device location for that feature.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Manual city search can be used instead, where available.

Only describe location access as optional or while-in-use if that matches the permissions and behavior of the published app.

3. Weather and Third-Party Services

Weathora uses third-party services to provide weather forecasts and geographic search results. These may include Open-Meteo's Forecast API and Geocoding API.

When you request a forecast, relevant coordinates may be sent to the weather provider. When you search for a city, your search query may be sent to the geocoding provider. Those providers process requests under their own policies and terms.

Open-Meteo: open-meteo.com

Weathora may use additional image, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services in future versions. This policy must be updated to identify any such services and explain their data practices before they are introduced.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the app may be used to:

  • Retrieve current weather and forecast information.
  • Find cities and display relevant weather results.
  • Remember app preferences or saved locations, if enabled.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability, if diagnostic tools are enabled.

We do not state that information is collected, stored, or used for analytics unless the corresponding feature is actually implemented.

5. Data Storage and Security

Some app preferences or cached information may be stored locally on your device, depending on the features enabled. Information sent to third-party providers is subject to their systems and policies.

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

Weathora does not sell personal information as part of its stated operating model. Data may be transmitted to weather and geocoding providers when needed to fulfill your request. Other sharing must be disclosed if advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other services are added.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Manage location permissions through your device settings.
  • Use manual city search instead of device location, where available.
  • Clear locally stored app data through the device settings, where supported.
  • Contact us with questions or requests about information associated with your use of Weathora.

Your legal rights may vary depending on your country or region. We will handle valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Weathora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature of the app, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
